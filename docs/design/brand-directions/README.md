# Prosody brand directions

Status: The owner selected **Current's identity with Frame's palette**, superseding the provisional Cadence preference expressed before the third board was visible. See [the refined board](../current-blue/brand-r1.png). These are brand explorations, not production logo assets.

The product brief is [RFC 0001](../../rfc/0001-prosody.md). Prosody is a private listening and viewing inbox for agent-generated material. The visual identity must suit text, images, audio, and video, with a minimal vertical feed and personal/team destinations.

| Direction | Point of view | Logo idea | Palette | Typography |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — Cadence | A personal journal that can speak: warm, literate, composed. | A lowercase p with an open bowl: a breath within a sentence. | Paper `#F4F0E8`, ink `#242820`, vermilion `#B84D35`, sage `#858D78`. | Contemporary editorial serif with humanist sans for controls. |
| 02 — Frame | A private gallery for useful information: clear, visual, precise. | An open geometric viewing frame with a descending p stem. | White `#F5F6F8`, black `#161A24`, ultramarine `#3154D9`, gray `#A8B2C4`. | Confident neo-grotesk, restrained weights, precise spacing. |
| 03 — Current | A calm stream you can carry with you: intimate, fluid, immersive. | A continuous folded stroke forming an open p-like loop. | Green-black `#142722`, mist `#EEF4F0`, mint `#B9DFC9`, river gray `#8DA49A`. | Open, softly geometric humanist sans. |

Each board explores a large wordmark and symbol, app icon, monochrome application, palette, type, and the same illustrative mobile content in light and dark treatments. The samples are for comparing identity; they do not prescribe final navigation or add product requirements.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Exact prompts are preserved in `r1/prompts.json`. Generated typography and marks are visual concepts: the chosen identity will need clean vector construction, exact font selection, contrast validation, and small-size checks before production. Palette hex values in this document are the proposed source values; image generation may approximate their rendered appearance.

## Selection and next stage

The selected direction preserves Current's flowing open-loop mark, soft geometric sans typography, and quiet composition. Use Frame's gallery white, near-black, ultramarine, and cool gray. Screen design proceeds from the RFC, covering onboarding/authentication, personal and team feeds, all media playback modes, source detail, progress, feed switching, agent connection/consent, BYOK setup, model/voice selection, team settings/membership, notifications, and loading/empty/error/recovery states.
